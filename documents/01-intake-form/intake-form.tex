\SetDocPurpose{%
This form is the starting point for asking the engineering center for help
with a project. It is meant to be filled out before any project charge code
exists and before any work begins. Its purpose is to describe, in plain
language, what you need help with, why you need it, and what you hope to
get out of it, so that the center can decide whether it is a good fit, ask
good follow-up questions, and avoid surprises later. Filling out this form
does not commit the center's time or resources, and it does not authorize
any engineering work to start. It simply starts a conversation on solid
footing. It is fine to leave something blank or mark it Unknown if you are
not yet sure -- the center will help fill in the gaps during discussion.%
}
