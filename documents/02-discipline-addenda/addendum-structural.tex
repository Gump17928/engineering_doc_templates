\SetDocPurpose{%
This addendum collects the structural-engineering detail that the
Pre-Project Intake and Discovery Form (Document 1) does not ask for. Fill
it out only if structural assistance was indicated on that form. Its
purpose is to give the center's structural engineers enough detail about
the structure, its condition, and known loading to scope the work
accurately. It is fine to mark an item Unknown -- the center will help
fill in technical gaps during discussion.%
}
