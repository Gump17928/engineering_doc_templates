\SetDocPurpose{%
This worksheet writes down the specific, numbered requirements this work
needs to satisfy, and how the center and the requester will know each one
has been met. Its purpose is to replace vague expectations with
requirements that are specific enough to design, build, and check against.
It is developed jointly by the requesting technical lead and the center
engineering lead, usually alongside the Scope Definition Worksheet
(Document 4). Marking a requirement Mandatory versus Preferred helps
everyone agree on what is truly required versus what is merely desired.%
}
