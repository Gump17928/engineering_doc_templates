\SetDocPurpose{%
This worksheet turns an accepted intake request into a clearly bounded
piece of work. Its purpose is to write down, in plain language, exactly
what the engineering center will and will not do, what the requester needs
to provide, what will be delivered, and who accepts the results. It is
developed jointly by the center lead and the requester after the Intake
Form (Document 1) has been reviewed. Once this record reaches Confirmed
status, it becomes the primary reference for preparing a cost/time
estimate and an eventual Statement of Work (SOW). Reaching Confirmed
status is not itself permission to start engineering work or to charge
project time -- that still requires the organization's normal funding and
authorization process.%
}
