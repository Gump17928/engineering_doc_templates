\SetDocPurpose{%
This document is the final check before a cost/time estimate and a
Statement of Work (SOW) are prepared. Its purpose is to confirm, in one
place, that the scope, requirements, and known constraints have been
reviewed, that remaining open issues are identified rather than hidden,
and that preliminary schedule and resource assumptions are reasonable.
Signing this confirmation marks the record as ready to support SOW
preparation -- it is not itself permission to begin engineering work or to
charge project time. That still requires the organization's normal
funding and authorization process.%
}
